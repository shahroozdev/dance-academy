**About Malhaar Dance Academy** — *Dance Better. Live Better.*
Join us at Malhaar Dance Academy to take advantage of our professional instructors, welcoming
community of dancers and our studio. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced dancer looking to
improve your technique, you can find it all at our exceptional Dance Academy. Reach out today to
learn more!

**Classes We Offer** — *Join Our Dance Community*
Our Dance Academy offers variety of dance styles and classes for all skill levels. Our instructors
are passionate about helping each dancer achieve their highest potential. Have a look at our class
schedule below to find the one that best fits your needs.

We aim to give our students the best training through our well-structured curriculum, and share
knowledge and experience of our rich Indian culture and traditions through our class. We make sure
we create an environment of disciplines, fun and values for our students.

- **Bharatanatyam** — An ancient Indian classical dance of India is taught. In this class, initially
  we teach the fundamental steps called the 'Adavus' to strengthen and firm their footsteps to
  improve on the rhythm, stamina, speed and body posture. Shlokas, Single hand gestures and double
  hand gestures will also be taught. Once their base is strong, they move to the next level to learn
  the bharatanatyam dance sequel.
- **Mohiniyattam** — This classical dance form is known for 'laasya' (graceful movements) and its
  subtle rendition. In this class, you will be taught the basic adavus along with the 'mudras' (hand
  gestures) and abhinaya.
- **Semi Classical Dance** — A combination of Bharatanatyam and other indian dance styles (which is
  less complex than pure classical dance) without losing the authenticity will be taught. This class
  will be interesting by including fusion music. Everyone loves the opportunity to explore the new
  dance moves taught in this class, while also mastering the classic moves. Our amazing instructors
  make sure everyone gets individual attention while keeping the class collaborative.
- **Bollywood** — This class will have you on your feet in no time. Many of our dancers love taking
  this class, as it gives them the opportunity to learn dance on Film songs.

## Page 2 — Our Instructors

**Meet the Team** — *You’re in Great Hands*
We believe in nurturing creativity and artistic development, and offer a warm and positive
environment to foster the growth that all dancers want and need. We make this possible with our
instructors.

The page is a slideshow showing one instructor at a time.

### Sreevidhya Vijayan

Sreevidhya is well-established dancer and choreographer who has been trained in Bharatanatyam,
Mohiniyattam and Kuchupudi. Sreevidhya began her career as a professional dancer at the very early
age of 13 and her performances were greatly appreciated by many prominent artists. She has worked
for various renowned artists and productions in Oman, India and the U.S.A. Some of her most
cherished opportunities and achievements are as follows:

- Winner at 'Natya Idol Champion 2016'
- Semi-Finalist in a Mohiniyattam Reality Show 'LAASYAM' in Amrita TV
- Performed at the popular Soorya Festival, 'Rhythm' chapter organized by Shri. Soorya Krishnamurthy
- Performed at her own production showing tribute to Indian classical dance, “Natya Pranam”, a two
  hour continuous show for art lovers
- Winner of ISC Minara Dance Festival, Muscat (2008, 2009, 2010 and 2011)

### Varsha Vijayan (Guest Instructor)

Varsha began her journey into the world of dance by getting trained in bharatanatyam. Her interest
towards dance made her explore different styles including Bharatanatyam, Kuchupudi, Bollywood and
Western. Some of her achievements are as follows:

- Runner-Up at Natya Idol Champion 2017
- Khafeef International Dance Festival 2013 – Popular Choice Winner
- Dance Mania: Solo Category Winner for three consecutive years for cinematic dance
- Center for Performing Arts: Best Dancer Award 2010
- Dance Muscat Dance 2009: Popular Choice Winner
- Muscat Festival's Got Talent – Runner up
